## Universal content rules

1. **Lead with the answer.** Headline says what; body explains why.
2. **No more than three adjectives in a paragraph.** Reach for stronger nouns and verbs first.
3. **Numbers are first-class.** Round in marketing (98%); precise in operator UI (98.2%).
4. **Sentence case everywhere.** Title Case is reserved for proper nouns.
5. **Past tense for done. Present continuous for ongoing.**
6. **No exclamation marks.** Two exceptions: brand statement and confirmation toasts where excitement is genuine.
7. **Reduce three things every revision.** Three adjectives, three sentences, three exclamation marks. Aggressive editing is the voice.
